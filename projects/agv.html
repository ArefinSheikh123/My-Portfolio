PROJECT 02 / INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION

Automated Guided Vehicle

A 100 kg payload platform, engineered around structural integrity, load distribution, and autonomous navigation.

CASE STUDY / 02—04

01 / THE PROJECT

Autonomy starts with a solid foundation.

As mechanical lead, I designed the structural chassis of a differential-drive Automated Guided Vehicle rated for a 100 kg payload. The platform was developed for autonomous material handling in industrial environments.

ANSYS structural studies explored payload shifts, sudden stops, and ramp traversal. Material selection and load distribution were refined to balance chassis strength, weight, and manufacturability.

The mechanical layout also supported SLAM-based navigation. Sensor mounting geometry, clear fields of view, and a low center of gravity were considered alongside the drive system and payload.
