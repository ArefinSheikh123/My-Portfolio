Mars Rover Chassis

CASE STUDY / 05—05

01 / THE PROJECT

Built for the terrain. Tested by competition.

As lead design engineer for the Mars rover chassis, I worked on a platform built to navigate challenging terrain at the Anatolian Rover Challenge 2023. The team earned fifth place globally.

SolidWorks structural design and FEA stress optimization guided the balance between durability and weight. Chassis geometry and material decisions were developed around field loads and terrain traversal.

The project brought together mechanical design, analysis, and practical integration in a competition environment. It reinforced my approach to engineering: a design has to work beyond the CAD screen.
