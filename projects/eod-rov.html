PROJECT 01 / ROBOTICS · MECHANISM DESIGN

Man-Portable EOD ROV

A compact, field-deployable robot. Custom drive systems, carbon-fiber structures, and mechanical components built in-house.

CASE STUDY / 01—04

01 / THE PROJECT

From concept to working hardware.

A man-portable Explosive Ordnance Disposal robot calls for a careful balance of compact packaging, structural strength, and reliable motion. I engineered and manufactured its mechanical components in-house, from the carbon-fiber frame to the custom drive system.

The drive system centers on a globoidal worm gearbox designed for high torque in a compact envelope. Tooth geometry, wear resistance, and manufacturing constraints informed the mechanism design.

Wet layup and vacuum bagging made lightweight composite structures possible within the local fabrication workflow. Designing the mechanism and the manufacturing process together helped turn CAD assemblies into functional hardware.
